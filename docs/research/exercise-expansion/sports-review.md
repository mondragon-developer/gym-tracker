# Boxing and soccer footage review

Reviewed November 6, 2026. Five clips selected for catalog IDs 290-294. The full source URLs, declared licenses, original hashes and excerpt timestamps are in [sport-selection.json](../../../scripts/enrichment/sport-selection.json). These are new, specific drills; existing generic boxing entries retain their IDs.

The originals and output clips were inspected using contact sheets. Selected output clips were sampled at three frames per second. The boxing originals are 320 x 224 pixels, with borders and a thin edge artifact. Preserve that source detail without upscaling or fabricating frames. Soccer footage is reduced to 720 pixels wide. Excerpts retain normal speed and orientation, with audio removed and a poster extracted. Bilingual descriptions and four-step practice instructions accompany each clip.

Pexels 9517738, a close-up juggling clip, was rejected because the crop hides some ball contacts. The previously reviewed generic Boxer.webm remains excluded because it does not isolate the requested drills. The 18 earlier coaching links remain references only, not permission to reuse their footage.
